# Privacy Policy — Anti Journal

**Effective date:** November 2026

AntiPerson ("we", "us", "our") operates the Anti Journal publication platform at https://theantijournals.web.app (the "Site"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, the legal basis for that use, and your rights regarding that information.

## 1. Data Controller

AntiPerson is the data controller of your personal data. You may contact us at hello@antiperson.example with any questions about this policy or your data.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Information you provide directly

- **Account information:** When you register, we collect your email address, full name, and password (managed by our authentication provider).
- **Payment information:** When you make a purchase or subscribe, payment details are handled by Paystack. We do not store your credit card number. Paystack provides us with a transaction reference and payment status only.
- **Profile information:** You may optionally provide a biography or avatar image.
- **Newsletter:** Your email address when you subscribe to our newsletter.

### Information collected automatically

- **Usage data:** We collect your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referring URL for analytics and security purposes, only with your cookie consent.
- **Cookies:** We use essential cookies for session management and, with your consent, analytics cookies for site preferences and usage measurement.

### Third-party services

- **Authentication provider:** Hosts our database and authentication. Your email and encrypted password are stored by the provider.
- **Paystack:** Processes all payments. Your payment details are handled entirely by Paystack.
- **Firebase Hosting:** Serves the static frontend. No personal data is stored by Firebase beyond standard hosting logs.

## 3. How We Use Your Information and Legal Basis

| Purpose | Data used | Legal basis |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Account creation and management | Email, name, password | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Payment processing | Transaction reference, payment status | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) |
| Newsletter delivery | Email address | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) |
| Analytics and security | IP address, browser, pages visited | Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — via cookie banner |
| Legal compliance and fraud prevention | Account and payment records | Legal obligation / legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(c) and (f) GDPR) |

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with:
- **Paystack** — for payment processing
- **Authentication provider** — for authentication and account hosting
- **Legal requirements** — if required by law or to protect rights

## 5. International Transfers

Our service providers are located in the United States and Nigeria. Where your data is transferred outside the European Union or Nigeria, we rely on standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision to ensure an adequate level of protection.

## 6. Your Rights

You may:
- Access, correct, or delete your personal data
- Withdraw consent for marketing emails at any time
- Export your data
- Object to processing based on legitimate interest
- Close your account (which deletes your personal data)
- Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise these rights, contact us at hello@antiperson.example.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain account data as long as your account is active. Payment records are retained for accounting and dispute resolution purposes. Analytics data is retained for 12 months and then deleted. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

## 8. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider. We do not store payment card details. Access to personal data is restricted to authorized personnel.

## 9. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a personal data breach that is likely to result in a risk to your rights and freedoms, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority without undue delay, and in any case within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. Where the breach is likely to result in a high risk to you, we will also notify you directly without undue delay.

## 10. Children's Privacy

The Site is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

## 11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes are effective when posted on the Site. We will notify you of material changes by email or via a prominent notice on the Site.
